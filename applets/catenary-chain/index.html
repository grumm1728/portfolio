Catenary Chain

Drag point B and change the length S to watch the lowest point of the chain move.

construction follows the catenary setup.

Matt Enlow - May 22, 2026

A chain has one end fixed at the origin, and the other end is free. When you place that other end at a particular point in the plane, the chain hangs, forming part of a catenary. What is the locus of the lowest point of the chain, as its free end varies?
